# CleanAir Retrofit Kit claims register

What the kit may say, what it may say only after accredited testing, and what it must never say. Method: STANDARDS section 25.5a. The kit reduces airborne particle exposure; it never makes a room safe.

Table 1. Claims by option.

| Option | May claim now (design intent) | Only after accredited testing | Never claim | Limits to state on the product |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CA2 filter cube | "Designed to reduce airborne particles"; "uses MERV-13 media (ASHRAE 52.2)" for panels bought with that rating; "targets a clean air delivery rate of 400 m3/h per cube"; "no UV, ioniser or ozone source" | A measured CADR (m3/h) for named particle sizes at a stated fan and speed; eACH for a named room; a measured bypass; an AHAM AC-1 style rating | That it purifies the air, sterilises, kills or eliminates germs or viruses, makes a room virus-free, is 100 % effective, removes all particles, prevents infection or transmission, is medical-grade, or protects against any disease; any HEPA claim beyond a bought filter's stated class | Not a substitute for ventilation, masks or IPC measures; performance falls as panels load; use only with a listed fan, guards in place, on the floor |
| CA1 collar and cassette | As CA2, per kit | As CA2 | As CA2; no UV-C claim (module removed) | As CA2; seal depends on the fan's guard shape |
| CA3 grille cassette | As CA2, while the air handler runs | As CA2 | As CA2 | Works only while the system fan runs; may reduce system airflow |

Table 2. Benchmarks cited as targets (not passes).

| Benchmark | Requirement | Applies to | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CDC "Aim for 5" | 5 or more ACH of clean air, ventilation and filtration added | Room planning (PR-002, PR-003) | Target |
| Lancet Commission NADR | 4, 6 and above 6 ACHe; 10 and 14 L/s per person | Room planning | Target |
| AHAM AC-1 | CADR for smoke, dust and pollen | V2 method | Target |
| ASHRAE 52.2 | MERV-13 E1 50 %, E2 85 %, E3 90 % minimum | Bought panels | Bought rating |
| UL 2998 | Ozone under 0.005 ppm | Whole kit | Target; no ozone source by design |
| EN 1822 and ISO 29463 | HEPA and EPA classes | Not used now | Not claimed |
| IEC 62471 and ACGIH TLV | UV exposure limits | Any future UV-C module (D5) | Not applicable now |

## On-product wording (proposed, awaiting Amish)

"Designed to reduce airborne particles. Use with a listed box fan, guards in place, on the floor. Not a substitute for ventilation, masks or infection prevention measures. Change all four panels by the date below or when the ribbon at the outlet barely moves. Unplug before changing panels. Keep 1 m from heaters and flames. CONCEPT PROTOTYPE: not for use with occupants until laboratory tested."

## Release limitation

No prototype is used to protect occupants until its delivered airflow, seal and filtration efficiency are measured by a qualified laboratory.
