# CleanAir Retrofit Kit project plan

The project is at TRL 3 on paper (stage P3). The next gate is the team's own: evidence that the seal and the filtration meet minimum thresholds, which at TRL 4 means a measured CADR, a measured bypass and a motor temperature check. P4 work is on hold under the portfolio cap and is listed here as planned work only.

## Purpose

Turn fans that facilities already have into room air cleaners designed to reduce airborne particles, at a known clean air delivery rate and a known cost, in field hospitals, shelters, clinics and communal spaces where new HVAC is not feasible.

## Kill criteria (project stop rules, not a product claim)

From CAR-PRB-001: stop or change course if, at TRL 4, the measured CADR of one kit on a typical site fan is below 300 m3/h; bypass above 15 % cannot be fixed without tools; motor temperature exceeds the fan's listing limit with the kit fitted; kit parts exceed USD 150 with no route to the target; or field partners cannot resupply filters.

## Targets and key performance indicators

| KPI | Target | Now (paper) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CADR per kit, 0.3 to 1 um, high speed | At least 400 m3/h | 398 to 459 m3/h measured on four-panel cubes |
| Kits for 5 ACH in a 150 m3 ward | 2 | 2 |
| Bypass | 10 % or less | 3.7 to 8.2 % at 1 mm |
| Assembly | 15 min, no tools | 8 to 12 min estimate |
| Kit cost | USD 150 (stretch under USD 80 at volume) | USD 111.83 |
| Cost of clean air | Under USD 0.30 per m3/h of CADR | USD 0.24 to 0.28 |

## 60 to 90 day plan once P4 opens

1. Days 1 to 15: Amish decides D1 to D3; buy parts for three kits; agree a laboratory.
2. Days 15 to 45: build three kits to CAR-BLD-001; fit test on five fan models (the team's own near-term goal); motor temperature flat (V5).
3. Days 45 to 75: laboratory CADR and bypass (V2, V4); loaded-flow test (V4); timed assembly with the personas (V6).
4. Days 75 to 90: first-pass BOM and unit cost for a batch of 50 kits (the team's goal); report against the decision gate.

## Support needed from Helpful Engineering

Mechanical support for the round-fan adaptor (D2), materials selection for fire and humidity (C2), air quality testing support (a particle counter and a chamber), and introductions to field partners and a MERV-13 bulk supplier, as the team deck asks.

## External partners to explore

A filtration testing laboratory (aerosol and particle count), field partners for site fan inventories (UNHCR and MSF named in the deck), a MERV-13 panel supplier for bulk supply, and FEMA for viability input.

## Next steps and decisions

Decision request to Amish: D1 (CA2 or CA1, within 10 %), D3 (filter-change target), then D2 and D4 to D7 (CAR-DEC-001). No further design work until D1 is decided.
