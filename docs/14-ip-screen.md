# CleanAir Retrofit Kit patent and freedom-to-operate screen

A first desk screen, **not legal advice**. A patent attorney gives the opinion before any product is made for sale or distribution. Method: STANDARDS section 25.7. Each patent was read on Google Patents on 2026-11-12; legal status is as Google Patents shows it, which Google itself notes is an assumption, not a legal conclusion. The closest live patent is 3M's US 12,582,929 B2 for a kit that assembles into a filter cube; CA2 differs from its claim 1 in having no hinged tabs in the floor or ceiling, but the attorney must confirm.
