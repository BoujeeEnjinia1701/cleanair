# 0001: Concept selection

Status: **proposed, awaiting Amish** (CAR-DEC-001 D1). CA2 and CA1 score within 10 %, so both go to Amish without a pick.

## Context

The team's iteration 2 (CA1) seals one flat MERV-13 cassette to an existing fan with a silicone collar. The STEM check shows that a box fan develops only 27 to 55 Pa, so one 0.25 m2 panel holds the fan to 13 to 71 % of its free air, and measured data give one filter on a box fan 29 to 35 % of the clean air of the same fan on a four-panel cube (CAR-STM-001 X1, X2).

## Options considered

- CA1 collar and flat cassette on a shrouded fan: 170 of 245; measured 131 to 141 m3/h per kit; 6 kits per 150 m3 ward; fits any shrouded fan 12 to 24 in; fastest filter change.
- CA2 filter cube on a box fan: 185; measured 398 to 459 m3/h; 2 cubes per ward; needs a box fan; four panels to change.
- CA3 return-grille cassette: 166; only for ducted buildings; a 2 in panel exceeds the 25 Pa DOE filter drop.

## Decision

None yet. Recommendation for Amish's weighing: CA2 for the main need (clean air per unit and per dollar), with CA1's collar kept as the round-fan adaptor (D2). CA2 is carried in the drawing set, BOM and build plan so that a constructable design exists at TRL 3; this is not a choice.

## Consequences

If Amish chooses CA1, the CA1 model (already at D3) becomes the drawing set, BOM and build plan, PR-001 to PR-003 are restated per kit, and the pitch counts more kits per room. If CA2, the pitch moves from "clip-on collar" to "filter cube kit", which is a pitch change and also his decision.
