# CleanAir Retrofit Kit feasibility assessment

The filter cube is feasible on paper: every part is bought or cut by hand, the physics is measured in published work, and the cost is under the target. The weakest points are the measured CADR's low end against PR-001, filter resupply, and whether a fan's safety listing covers this use.

Table 1. Feasibility by aspect.

| Aspect | Assessment | Rating | Risks and notes | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Design | Four panels keep the face velocity at 0.20 to 0.65 m/s within a box fan's 27 to 55 Pa | High | Model brackets the measurements and runs high | CAR-STM-001 S1 to S3; CAR-CAL-001 sections 3 to 5 |
| Performance | Measured four-panel CADR 398 to 459 m3/h; 2 cubes per 150 m3 ward | Medium | PR-001 at risk at the low end; in-use efficiency 39 to 58 % | CAR-CAL-001 sections 5 and 6 |
| Materials | Corrugated PP, EPDM foam, card-framed synthetic media | Medium | Card frames in humidity; combustibility | CAR-STM-001 S16, S22 |
| Manufacturing and assembly | Knife, straight edge, drill, a printer for the feet | High | Score depth consistency on twin-wall sheet | CAR-BLD-001 |
| Power | No kit electrics; fan 52.8 to 103 W | High | 230 V, 50 Hz fans to check | CAR-STM-001 S9, S30 |
| Controls | None; the fan's switch | High | Users may run on low for noise (D7) | CAR-STM-001 S26 |
| Supply | Standard 20 x 20 x 2 in MERV-13 panels sold widely in North America | Medium | Availability and price in target countries to assess with field partners | CAR-MKT-001 |
| Regulatory | No medical claim; fan listing carries electrical safety | Medium | Listing coverage for this use to assess | CAR-REG-001 R1, R2 |
| Freedom to operate | Close to US 12,582,929 B2 (3M), without its hinged tabs | Medium | Attorney opinion needed | CAR-FTO-001 |
