# CleanAir Retrofit Kit STEM check

Verdict: the filter cube (option CA2) sits within known science, and the measured four-panel Corsi-Rosenthal data are its design basis: **398 to 459 m3/h of clean air delivery per cube on high** (EPA ORD and UC Davis), so a 150 m3 ward needs **two cubes** for CDC's 5 ACH (5.3 to 6.1 eACH) and a 60 m3 clinic room needs one (6.6 to 7.6 eACH). A box fan develops only about 27 to 55 Pa at shut-off; spread over four panels (0.98 m2) the face velocity is 0.20 to 0.65 m/s and the drop 9 to 34 Pa, which the fan can supply. The team's flat-cassette concept (CA1) puts all the flow through one 0.25 m2 panel: at the fan's free-air velocity that panel would need 133 to 215 Pa, so the fan slows to 13 to 71 % of its free air, and one filter on a box fan was measured at 29 to 35 % of the same fan on a cube. Two details of the team concept fail and were fixed under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23: a collar cannot seal to an unshrouded ceiling fan (scope now shrouded fans and duct openings, F1), and a low-cost UV-C strip at the fan face gives about 0.003 mJ/cm2, a 1 % single-pass effect (module removed, F2). The independent review added a third fix (F3: hold-down straps on the fan frame, not the guard). Of 32 rows for CA2, 16 are within known science, 15 plausible and waiting for TRL 4 evidence, and one (S18, a 2 minute filter change for the whole set) fails with a fix that changes a user-facing target, so it goes to Amish (CAR-DEC-001 D3). `stem_check: open`. Method: STANDARDS sections 20 and 25.6a; calculations in `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py` and `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` (CAR-CAL-001). The kit describes risk reduction only (CAR-CLM-001).

## Concept options

- **CA2 filter cube:** no Fails row; S18 fails with a fix that goes to Amish (D3). Carried in the drawing set; the choice between CA2 and CA1 is Amish's (scores within 10 %, CAR-OPT-001).
- **CA1 collar and flat cassette (team concept):** working principle holds, at about a third of CA2's measured CADR on the same fan (X1, X2). Ceiling-fan scope (X3) and the UV-C strip (X4) fixed under the standing instruction.

## Fixes made under the standing instruction (2026-10-23)

Each keeps the working principle (an existing fan drives air through bought MERV-13 media) and is recorded in CAR-DEC-001 and CAR-DDR-003.

- **F1 Scope: shrouded fans and duct openings.** Ceiling fans (29 to 54 in spans, no shroud, almost no static pressure) cannot be sealed to by a 12 to 24 in collar; they are removed from the scope (X3).
- **F2 UV-C strip removed.** A low-cost strip gives about 0.003 mJ/cm2 (1 % single pass, +0.07 eACH in the ward); even an 18 W high-power strip reaches about 0.16 mJ/cm2 with a generous path, and its LEDs reach the ACGIH eye limit in 7 to 11 minutes at 1 m. Dose far too low for its cost and hazard; removed from every option (X4). An enclosed, interlocked UV-C module would be a separate product (CAR-DEC-001 D5).
- **F3 Hold-down straps on the fan frame.** The independent review found that straps across the outlet guard could press it toward the blades; they now cross the solid frame 261 mm from the centre, outside the 484 mm guard (S23).

## Independent review

A separate agent, run as its own process on 2026-11-12 with only the claims list, the sources and the model assumptions (not this document's reasoning), tried to refute every "Within known science" verdict. Of 24 such rows, 9 held (S11, S12, S14, S15, S23, X2, X3, X5, X13), 14 were weak (S1, S2, S3, S5, S6, S7, S9, S20, S24, X1, X4, X6, X8, X9) and 1 was refuted (S19), and it listed 12 missed claims. What changed:

- **S1 (weak):** the 27.5 Pa shut-off came from a low-speed point and was used with high-speed flows. Both values are now carried (27.5 Pa and 55 Pa by the fan laws) and every flow is a range over both.
- **S2 (weak):** the order-of-magnitude check was circular (the measured point was used to fit the curve). Restated as a bracketing model, verdict now plausible; measured CADR is the design basis.
- **S3, S5, S6 (weak):** the five-panel Dal Porto data are not CA2. The CA2 range is now 398 to 459 m3/h (four panels), the ward needs two cubes, not one, and S6 notes low speed and loading.
- **S7 (weak):** the bypass was the most favourable case and missed the tray seam. Now the worst case over fans and media on 6.2 m of seam: 3.7 to 8.2 % at 1 mm.
- **S9 (weak):** listing limits sit below the 105 C class rating, and the tests had the fan upright. Verdict now plausible; S25 added for the flat orientation.
- **S19 (refuted):** 15 m3/h per W was a five-panel figure. Corrected to about 4 m3/h per W for four panels (14.8 for the five-panel reference).
- **S23 (holds, with a gap):** strap tension on the outlet guard; fixed by F3.
- **X1, X6, X9, X12 (weak):** X1 now carries both shut-off pressures; X6 adds the box fan's 2.15 m face (up to 31 % at 5 mm); X9 adds UL's 7.3 times loading and is now plausible; X12 is restated as failing against the DOE figure, which is an optimum, not a tolerance.
- **X4 (weak):** the high-power dose used a longer path than the low-cost case; both paths are now stated, and the eye limit is 7 to 11 minutes for 40 to 65 mW LEDs (it was given for 40 mW only).
- **S20, S24, X8 (weak):** S20 now states that the fan is excluded and the yearly filter cost is in CAR-MKT-001; S24 now says the spread is model uncertainty on one fan, not two fan models; X8 notes that humidity, compression set and disinfectant resistance of the silicone are not covered by the listing.
- **S18 (plausible, flagged):** contradicted by its own check; now "Fails, fix proposed", awaiting Amish (D3).
- **Missed claims added as S25 to S32:** flat fan orientation, low speed and noise, room mixing, the Lancet per-person criterion, used-panel handling, 230 V and 50 Hz fans, eACH adding to ventilation, and the fan-to-deck seal.
