# CleanAir Retrofit Kit user requirements

What the users in CAR-PRB-001 need, written from their side. Priorities are proposals for review with a field partner.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Performance | Enough clean air | I need to reach about 5 air changes per hour of clean air in a ward or clinic room with a small, known number of kits | High | Facility manager (P1); CDC, Lancet Commission targets |
| UR-002 | Deployment and assembly | Use what is on site | I need to use fans or duct openings we already have, with no new HVAC or permanent change | High | Team deck; humanitarian operator (P3) |
| UR-003 | Performance | Catch respiratory-size particles | The kit must catch particles of about 0.3 to 3 um, using media with a known bought rating | High | Team deck (MERV-13); healthcare worker (P2) |
| UR-004 | Deployment and assembly | Quick, tool-free set-up | I need to assemble it without tools in under 15 minutes | High | Team deck; volunteer (P5) |
| UR-005 | Maintenance | Quick filter change | I need to change filters in minutes, with minimal training, and handle used filters safely | High | Team deck (under 2 minutes); volunteer (P5) |
| UR-006 | Safety | No new hazard | The kit must not add an electrical, UV, ozone or pinch hazard, or defeat the fan's guards | High | Helpful Engineering; occupants (P4) |
| UR-007 | Safety | Stays put | It must not tip over in a busy ward | Medium | Ward nurse (P2) |
| UR-008 | Usability | Know when to act | I need to know when filters need changing and whether the kit is running | Medium | Facility manager (P1); team non-technical risks |
| UR-009 | Maintenance | Wipe clean | Its surfaces must wipe clean with standard disinfectants | Medium | Team deck |
| UR-010 | Cost | Affordable kit | A complete kit must cost no more than about USD 150, and much less at volume | High | Team deck (target USD 150, stretch under USD 80) |
| UR-011 | Logistics | Standard consumables, flat pack | Filters must be a standard size we can buy locally, and the kit must pack flat | High | Logistics officer (P3); team supply-chain risk |
| UR-012 | Environment and durability | Heat and humidity | It must hold up in hot, humid field sites | Medium | Team technical risks |
| UR-013 | Usability | Tolerable noise | It must be quiet enough to run in a ward at night | Medium | Patient (P4) |
| UR-014 | Regulatory | Honest and lawful | Claims must be honest and the kit must not breach fan, appliance or ozone rules | High | Helpful Engineering; regulators |
| UR-015 | Performance | Low power | It must run on site power or a generator without a large load | Medium | Humanitarian operator (P3) |
| UR-016 | Deployment and assembly | Fits common fans | It should fit the fans found in the field, 12 to 24 in across, and duct openings | Medium | Team deck |
